# Wellness Without Borders 🌍💚

**Status:** Retired  

Wellness Without Borders was a nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting mental, physical, and emotional wellness across communities worldwide. Our mission was simple: **make wellness accessible to everyone, everywhere**.
Succesfully raised 90% of our **$1000** goal!

---

## Mission

We believed that **health and well-being are universal rights**, not privileges. By providing resources, guidance, and community support, we aimed to break barriers and empower individuals to live healthier, happier lives.

---

## Programs & Initiatives

During its operation, Wellness Without Borders focused on:

- **Community Programs:** Workshops, support groups, and wellness events  
- **Online Resources:** Guides, tips, and educational materials on mental and physical health  
- **Partnerships:** Collaborations with organizations to expand access to wellness  
- **Advocacy & Awareness:** Promoting holistic health initiatives and destigmatizing mental health  

---

## Website & Digital Resources

This repository preserves the **website and digital resources** built to support the nonprofit’s mission. The site was designed to:

- Be fully accessible and mobile-friendly  
- Provide clear, easy-to-navigate wellness resources  
- Serve as a hub for programs, events, and community engagement  

---

## Retirement Notice

As of now, **Wellness Without Borders is officially retired**. While the nonprofit is no longer active, its legacy of community wellness, education, and advocacy lives on through this repository.
